hs-money

hs-money is a private, single-user tool that I (Worakan Jeeprabnan) use to keep my own expense receipts and work-claim PDFs for Hispeed Racing Team in my own Google Drive (kemkmitl@gmail.com).

  • It runs on my own server.
  • It uses the Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets APIs only to upload and organize my own files in my own Google Drive.
  • It does not share data with any third party.
  • It is not offered to, or usable by, other users.
