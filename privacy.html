Privacy Policy – hs-money

Last updated: October 29, 2026

hs-money is a private, single-user tool operated by Worakan Jeeprabnan for his own use. It is not offered to other users. This policy explains how it handles data.

What data the app accesses

The app accesses only files in the owner's own Google Drive (kemkmitl@gmail.com) that the app itself creates or organizes: expense receipts, work-claim PDFs, and related Google Docs and Google Sheets for Hispeed Racing Team. It does not access any other user's data.

How the data is used

The Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets APIs are used only to upload, name, organize and update these files in the owner's Drive. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage

Files are stored in the owner's Google Drive. The app runs on the owner's own private server, which may keep temporary working copies and the OAuth credentials needed to access the owner's Drive. These are not exposed publicly.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files created by the app remain in the owner's Google Drive and can be deleted there at any time.

Contact

Questions: kemkmitl@gmail.com
